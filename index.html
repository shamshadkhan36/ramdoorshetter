6-Step Manufacturing Process

HOW WE BUILD UNBREAKABLE SHUTTERS

Every shutter slat is rolled, treated, and tested in our high-capacity fabrication plant under strict quality assurance standards.

01

Prime Galvanized Steel Coils

We exclusively procure certified Tata Steel / JSW high-tensile cold-rolled galvanized steel coils with heavy zinc coating (120-275 GSM) to eliminate rust.

02

Precision CNC Roll Forming

Computerized 14-stage roll forming lines shape double-rib profile slats with deep interlocking knuckles that prevent curtain sagging under high wind loads.

03

200°C Electrostatic Powder Coat

Automated 7-tank chemical pre-treatment followed by pure polyester thermosetting powder coating cured at 200°C for superior weather & scratch resistance.

04

High-Torque Drive Motor Fitment

Coupled with 100% copper-wound Italian and German drive motors equipped with thermal overload cut-off, manual chain override, and dual remote transmitters.

05

Multi-Point Quality Testing

Each shutter curtain undergoes 50+ dry test cycles on our vertical factory test rigs to verify balance, smooth slat travel, motor torque, and emergency braking.

06

Site Delivery & Expert Fitment

Delivered in dedicated protective wrapping and installed by our certified master technicians with same-day handover and structural warranty certificate.

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What happens to a motorized rolling shutter during a power outage?

All our motorized rolling shutters are equipped with a built-in manual override chain mechanism or spring release. In case of electricity failure, you can effortlessly open or close the shutter manually. We also offer dedicated UPS battery backups that provide 20+ motorized cycles during power cuts.

How fast can you fabricate and install a custom-sized shutter?

Because we operate our own in-house slitting and roll-forming plant, standard sizes can be fabricated and installed within 24 to 48 hours. Custom powder-coated or heavy industrial orders typically take 3 to 5 business days.

Can you convert my existing manual hand-operated shutter to motorized?

Yes! We specialize in motorized retrofitting. Our technicians can install a center motor or heavy-duty side motor onto your existing rolling shaft within approximately 3-4 hours without disturbing your storefront frame.

What warranty do you provide on RAM Door Shutters?

We provide a 10-Year Structural & Anti-Corrosion Warranty on our galvanized steel slats and a 5-Year Full Replacement Warranty on Italian/German drive motors and electrical keyfob units.
